## Strategy Description

The ReSolve Trend Replication Program offers investors a way to access trend following strategies with historically strong returns and low correlation to traditional assets.

### Key Benefits

- Sophisticated replication of leading Trend Following strategies using machine learning techniques
- Highly diversified exposure across global markets and asset classes to pursue returns in both up and down markets
- Two complementary approaches combining top-down portfolio construction with bottom-up strategy selection
- Potential to reduce overall portfolio volatility and provide downside protection during equity market drawdowns
- Systematic, rules-based approach removing emotion and behavioral biases

The program aims to make trend following strategy more accessible through a robust, cost-effective solution designed to replicate leading trend following managers' performance.
