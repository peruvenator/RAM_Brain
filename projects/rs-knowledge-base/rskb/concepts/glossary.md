# Glossary

Key terms used across the Return Stacked® lineup and supporting content.

**Alpha** — A strategy's ability to beat the market; a return source not explained by exposure to broad market beta.

**Beta** — Volatility / systematic risk of a security or portfolio relative to the broad market.

**Carry / Roll Yield** — The expected return of an asset assuming no change in its price; the second source of return after price appreciation. Also called "yield."

**Cayman Subsidiary (CFC)** — A wholly-owned offshore subsidiary used by U.S. ETFs to hold commodity futures positions. Required for RIC qualifying-income tax compliance under the Internal Revenue Code. Not registered under the 1940 Act.

**Commodity** — A basic interchangeable good. Investors gain exposure via spot markets or derivatives (futures, options).

**Currency** — Generally accepted forms of payment issued by governments. Currency futures provide leveraged exposure to FX moves.

**Diversification** — A risk management strategy creating a mix of investments within a portfolio. *Diversification does not assure a profit.*

**Drawdown** — Peak-to-trough decline of an asset, investment, or fund over a specified period, typically expressed as a percentage.

**Effective Duration** — A bond portfolio's price sensitivity to interest rate changes. RSBT and RSBY's bond sleeves target the duration of the Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index (~6 years).

**Foreside Fund Services, LLC** — Distributor for the U.S. Return Stacked® ETF lineup and for Quantify ETFs. FINRA/SIPC member.

**Futures Contract** — Standardized exchange-traded contract obligating buyers to purchase, and sellers to sell, a specific quantity of an underlying asset at a predetermined price on a future date. Provides notional exposure with margin instead of full capital.

**Global Futures Markets** — Standardized futures contracts across international exchanges allowing buyers and sellers to transact in commodities, currencies, equities, and fixed income for delivery at a future date.

**Inflation Hedging** — Protection against the erosion of purchasing power. Strategies like trend, carry, and gold/bitcoin have historically exhibited inflation-hedging characteristics under different conditions.

**iShares S&P 500 ETF (IVV)** — ETF tracking the S&P 500 Index; representative U.S. equity sleeve building block.

**LongPoint Asset Management Inc.** — Investment Fund Manager for the Canadian Return Stacked® ETFs (RGBM, RGBM.U).

**Managed Futures** — Actively managed portfolios of futures contracts. The Return Stacked® lineup uses *trend-following* managed futures specifically.

**Merger Arbitrage** — Strategy that invests in companies involved in announced mergers and acquisitions, profiting from the spread between trading price and deal price.

**MER (Management Expense Ratio)** — Canadian equivalent of the U.S. expense ratio. Includes management fee, operating expenses, and applicable taxes.

**NAV** — Net Asset Value per share. The sum of fund assets minus liabilities, divided by shares outstanding.

**Newfound Research LLC** — Investment Sub-Adviser to the U.S. Return Stacked® ETFs and the RDMIX mutual fund (Balanced Allocation sleeve). Co-Promoter of the Canadian ETFs. Co-licensor of the "STKd" brand to Quantify.

**Notional Exposure** — The total face value of a position controlled via derivatives, regardless of how much capital is committed. A futures contract with $1 of margin can provide $10+ of notional exposure.

**Portable Alpha** — Institutional investing technique where an alpha-generating strategy is "ported" onto a beta exposure using leverage. Direct intellectual ancestor of return stacking.

**Quantify Chaos Advisors, LLC** — Adviser for the Quantify partner ETFs (BTGD, ISBG, ISSB), which use the "STKd" brand under license.

**Rational Advisors, Inc.** — Investment adviser for the RDMIX mutual fund.

**ReSolve Asset Management Inc. ("ReSolve Canada")** — Portfolio Manager for the Canadian ETFs (RGBM, RGBM.U) and Sub-Adviser of the Systematic Macro sleeve in RDMIX.

**ReSolve Asset Management SEZC (Cayman) ("ReSolve Global")** — Cayman-domiciled affiliate. Portfolio Sub-Advisor for the Canadian ETFs; Futures Trading Advisor for the U.S. Return Stacked® ETFs and RDMIX. Co-licensor of the "STKd" brand.

**RIC (Regulated Investment Company)** — U.S. tax classification for investment funds. Requires that 90%+ of gross income come from qualifying sources, which is why commodity-futures exposure is held in a Cayman Subsidiary.

**Risk Parity** — Portfolio construction approach where each holding contributes equal risk (volatility) rather than equal capital. Used in the RSSX gold/bitcoin sleeve.

**Roll Yield** — See Carry.

**Stack / Stacking** — Adding a diversifying return stream on top of a core exposure to achieve >100% notional per $1 of NAV. The defining mechanic of the Return Stacked® lineup.

**STKd** — Branded mark used by Quantify Funds, derivative of Return Stacked®, used under license.

**Systematic Macro** — A diversified futures strategy using multiple quantitative signals (momentum, trend, carry, seasonality, mean reversion) across global asset classes. Distinct from pure trend or pure carry; combines them with additional inputs. Used in the Canadian ETFs (RGBM/RGBM.U) and RDMIX.

**Tail Risk** — The possibility of extreme, low-probability events that can cause significant losses.

**Tidal Investments, LLC** — Investment Adviser for the U.S. Return Stacked® ETF lineup.

**Traditional Asset Class** — Stocks and bonds (i.e., the conventional 60/40 building blocks).

**Trend Following** — Strategy that buys assets moving up in price and sells/shorts assets moving down. Implementation typically uses moving averages, breakouts, and time-series momentum signals.

**U.S. Aggregate Bond ETF (AGG)** — ETF tracking the Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index; broad U.S. investment-grade bond exposure.

**U.S. 10-yr Treasury Futures (TY)** — Standardized futures contracts representing future obligation to buy/sell U.S. 10-Year Treasury notes.

**Volatility** — Degree of variation in an asset's price over time. Often expressed as annualized standard deviation.
