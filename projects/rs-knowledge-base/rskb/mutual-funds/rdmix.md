## Investment case

For every $1 invested, RDMIX seeks to provide **$1 of a U.S. balanced allocation strategy** and **$1 of a systematic macro strategy**.

Three pillars:
1. **Capital Efficiency** — simultaneous $1 + $1 exposure.
2. **Diversification** — systematic macro has historically exhibited low correlation to both stocks and bonds.
3. **Inflation Hedging** — long/short across global futures markets has historically exhibited inflation-hedging characteristics.

## Mechanics

### Balanced Allocation sleeve ($1) — approximately 50% U.S. equities / 50% U.S. bonds
- **Equity portion:** holds U.S. equities, U.S. equity ETFs (typically IVV — iShares S&P 500 ETF), and/or U.S. equity index futures
- **Bond portion:** holds U.S. Treasuries, U.S. bond ETFs (e.g., AGG), and/or U.S. Treasury futures (e.g., 10-Year TY)

**Example construction:**
- 50% iShares S&P 500 ETF (IVV)
- 25% U.S. Aggregate Bond ETF (AGG)
- 25% U.S. 10-Year Treasury futures (TY)

### Systematic Macro sleeve ($1)
Invests long and short across equities, bonds, currencies, and commodities using a variety of quantitative investment signals (momentum, trends, seasonal patterns, carry, mean reversion, others). Targets a range of annualized portfolio volatility to control tail risks.

The macro stack will also hold long or short positions in U.S. equity index futures and Treasury futures, increasing or decreasing the net exposure of the balanced sleeve.

### Operational details
- **Rebalances:** daily
- **Wrapper-specific:** as a mutual fund, RDMIX is purchased at NAV (or NAV + sales load for A shares); does not trade intraday like an ETF

## FAQs (from product page)

### How often does RDMIX rebalance?
Daily.

### How does RDMIX get its exposure to U.S. equities and bonds?
The U.S. equity portion can hold U.S. equities, U.S. equity ETFs, and U.S. equity index futures, or any combination. The U.S. bond portion can hold U.S. bonds, U.S. bond ETFs, and U.S. Treasury futures. The systematic macro strategy will also have long or short positions in U.S. equity index futures and Treasury futures.

### What is a systematic global macro strategy?
Same FAQ language as RGBM/RGBM.U: invests in futures contracts to gain dynamic exposure across global equity indices, fixed income, currencies, and commodities. Uses proprietary quantitative innovations including momentum, trends, seasonal patterns, carry, mean reversion, and others. Targets annualized portfolio volatility range.

### Is RDMIX tax efficient?
Daily gains and losses in futures contracts may be calculated as realized for tax purposes. Consult a tax advisor.

### How often does RDMIX make distributions?
Annually at calendar year-end, if any. Investors can pre-elect to have distributions reinvested.
