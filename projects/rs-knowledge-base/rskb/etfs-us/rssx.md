## Investment case

For every $1 invested, RSSX is designed to provide **$1 of large-cap U.S. equity exposure** and **$1 of a Gold/Bitcoin strategy**.

Three pillars:

1. **Capital Efficiency** — simultaneous $1 + $1 exposure to U.S. stocks plus a Gold/Bitcoin strategy.
2. **Diversification** — both gold and bitcoin have historically exhibited low correlations to traditional assets.
3. **Hard Asset Theme** — RSSX incorporates gold and bitcoin in a single strategy, offering access to assets that share characteristics often associated with **scarcity** and **store-of-value potential**.

## Mechanics

### U.S. Equity sleeve ($1)
Holds U.S. equities, U.S. equity ETFs (typically SPYM), and/or S&P 500 E-Mini futures.

**Example construction:** 70% SPYM + 30% S&P 500 E-Mini futures.

### Gold/Bitcoin sleeve ($1) — risk-parity construction
The defining feature of RSSX's stack. Rather than allocating equally by capital, the sleeve uses a **risk parity framework** designed so that gold and bitcoin contribute *equal volatility* to the portfolio.

Mechanics:
- Gold and bitcoin have very different volatility profiles (bitcoin ~3-5x more volatile than gold)
- To balance their risk contributions, the strategy allocates a **larger capital weight to the lower-volatility asset (gold)** and a **smaller capital weight to the higher-volatility asset (bitcoin)**
- Allocation is dynamic — adjusted as forecasted volatility changes

**Implementation:**
- Gold exposure: predominantly via gold futures (e.g., Micro Gold contracts)
- Bitcoin exposure: predominantly via a mix of ETFs (e.g., IBIT — iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF), ETPs, and CME bitcoin futures
- Does **not** invest directly in spot bitcoin, digital assets, or gold bullion. Investors seeking direct exposure should consider other products.

### Operational details
- **Rebalances:** daily
- **Cayman Subsidiary** used for futures positions

## FAQs (from product page)

### How does RSSX get its exposure to U.S. equities?
Holdings can include U.S. equities, U.S. equity ETFs, and U.S. equity index futures. Example: 70% large-cap equity ETF + 30% S&P 500 E-Mini futures.

### How does RSSX get its exposure to Gold and Bitcoin?
Through Gold ETFs and ETPs, Bitcoin ETFs and ETPs, Gold futures, and/or Bitcoin futures. Predominantly: gold via futures; bitcoin via a mixture of ETFs, ETPs, and futures.

### What is the Gold/Bitcoin strategy?
Employs a risk parity framework designed to balance the contribution of volatility from both gold and bitcoin. The allocation between gold and bitcoin is dynamically adjusted so that each asset contributes equally to the overall portfolio risk. The strategy will typically allocate a larger percentage of its assets to the asset class with lower forecasted volatility (gold).

### How often does RSSX make distributions?
Annually at calendar year-end, if any.
