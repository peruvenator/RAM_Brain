## What makes RSSB different

RSSB is the **capital-efficient core** of the lineup, positioned distinctly from the other Stacks.

While other Stacks add a *diversifier* on top of one core asset class (e.g., RSST = stocks + trend), **RSSB delivers $1 of stocks + $1 of bonds — the traditional 60/40 building blocks themselves — in a single ticker.** This frees up balance-sheet space for advisors to layer the diversifying Stacks on top within the same client account, achieving the full institutional-style "double stack."

## Investment case

Three pillars (different framing from the other Stacks):

1. **Capital Efficiency and Diversification** — replacing core stock and bond exposure with RSSB *frees up capital* to invest in diversifying asset classes and strategies.
2. **Reduce Cash Drag** — utilize embedded capital efficiency to hold cash without losing core stock and bond exposure.
3. **Avoiding 100% Equities for Growth Clients** — reduce equity concentration for growth-oriented clients by introducing a second, potentially diversifying source of returns (Treasuries) without giving up equity exposure.

## Mechanics

### Global Equity sleeve ($1)
Holds individual global equities, global equity ETFs, regional equity ETFs, and/or equity index futures.

**Example construction:**
- 50% SPDR Portfolio S&P 1500 Composite Stock Market ETF (SPTM)
- 40% Vanguard Total International Stock ETF (VXUS)
- 10% S&P 500 E-Mini futures

### U.S. Treasury Futures sleeve ($1)
Provides exposure to the U.S. Treasury bond market via Treasury futures with maturities ranging from 2 to 30 years.

**Approximate construction (current holdings):**
- 25% U.S. 2-Year Treasury futures
- 25% U.S. 5-Year Treasury futures
- 25% U.S. 10-Year Treasury futures
- 25% U.S. Long Bond futures

### Operational details
- **Rebalances:** 5% drift thresholds, evaluated daily
- **No Cayman Subsidiary** — no commodity futures held, so no qualifying-income concerns
- This is also why **RSSB has the lowest expense ratio in the suite** (0.40% net) — it's a simpler structure to operate

## FAQs (from product page)

### How does RSSB get its exposure to global equities?
Holdings can include global equities, global equity ETFs, regional equity ETFs, and equity index futures. Example: 50% large-cap U.S. equity ETF + 40% international ex-U.S. equity ETF + 10% S&P 500 E-Mini futures.

### How often does RSSB rebalance?
RSSB employs 5% drift thresholds to trigger portfolio rebalances. Thresholds are evaluated daily.

### Is RSSB tax efficient?
Daily gains and losses in futures contracts may be calculated as realized for tax purposes, which may affect capital gains depending upon the contract.

### How often does RSSB make distributions?
Annually at calendar year-end, if any.

## How advisors use RSSB

The strategically powerful use case: **double stacking within one account.**

Hold RSSB as the core (200% notional 60/40 in one ticker) and use the freed-up capital to buy diversifying Stacks (RSST, RSBT, RSSY, RSBY, RSBA, RSSX) on top. This lets a single client account achieve institutional-style portable alpha exposure without leaving the daily-liquid ETF wrapper.
