## Investment case

For every $1 invested, RSBY is designed to provide **$1 of broad U.S. bond exposure** and **$1 of a futures yield (carry) strategy**.

Three pillars:
1. **Capital Efficiency** — simultaneous $1 + $1 exposure to bonds and carry.
2. **Diversification** — futures yield has historically exhibited low average correlations to both stocks and bonds.
3. **Directional Alternative** — long/short ability gives carry the potential to dynamically respond to changing market regimes and complement directional alternative strategies like trend-following.

## Mechanics

### Bond sleeve ($1)
Identical construction to RSBT bond sleeve:
- Holds U.S. Treasuries, broad-based bond ETFs (e.g. SPAB), and/or U.S. Treasury futures
- **Example:** 75% SPAB + 6.25% each in 2Y/5Y/10Y/Long Bond Treasury futures
- Effective duration approximates Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index

### Futures Yield (Carry) sleeve ($1)
Identical methodology to RSSY's carry stack. See `/concepts/carry-yield.md`.

The carry stack will also hold long or short U.S. Treasury futures, dynamically increasing or decreasing the fund's effective duration based on the carry score for bonds.

### Operational details
- **Rebalances:** daily
- **Cayman Subsidiary** used

## FAQs (from product page)

### How does RSBY get its exposure to bonds?
Holdings can include U.S. Treasuries, bond ETFs, and U.S. Treasury futures, or any combination. Example: 75% broad U.S. bond ETF + 25% Treasury futures ladder. The Futures Yield strategy will also have long/short positions in Treasury futures.

### What is the effective duration of the Bond strategy?
Approximately tracks the Bloomberg U.S. Aggregate Bond Index.

### What is Futures Yield?
See `/concepts/carry-yield.md` and the same FAQ in `rssy.md`.

### Is RSBY tax efficient?
Daily gains and losses in futures may be realized for tax purposes; consult a tax advisor.

### How often does RSBY make distributions?
Annually at year-end, if any.
