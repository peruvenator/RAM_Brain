## Wrapper note

RGBM is technically an **alternative mutual fund** under Canadian regulations. Alternative mutual funds are permitted to use leverage and derivatives in ways traditional Canadian mutual funds cannot. RGBM uses leverage and derivatives to stack the returns of a global balanced strategy with those of a systematic macro strategy.

## Investment case

For every $1 invested, RGBM aims to provide **$1 of exposure to a global balanced allocation strategy** and **$1 of exposure to a systematic macro strategy**.

Four pillars (note: Canadian product page emphasizes a fourth pillar that the U.S. lineup doesn't):

1. **Capital Efficiency** — simultaneous $1 + $1 exposure.
2. **Diversification** — systematic macro has historically exhibited low correlation to both stocks and bonds.
3. **Inflation Hedging** — long/short across global futures markets has generally exhibited some inflation-hedging benefits.
4. **Tax Efficiency** — RGBM shareholders are not expected to receive any taxable distributions (corporate-class structure). For non-registered Canadian investors, this is a meaningful tax savings vs. distribution-paying funds.

## Mechanics

### Global Balanced Allocation sleeve ($1) — 50/50 stocks/bonds
- **Global equity portion** (~50% of the $1 sleeve): holds global equities, global equity ETFs, and/or global equity index futures. Example: 50% Vanguard Total World Stock ETF (VT)
- **Canadian bond portion** (~50% of the $1 sleeve): holds Canadian bonds, Canadian bond ETFs, and/or Canadian Government Bond futures. Example: 25% Vanguard Canadian Aggregate Bond ETF (VAB.TO) + 25% Canadian 10Y Government Bond futures (CGB)

### Systematic Macro sleeve ($1)
Invests in futures contracts to gain dynamic exposure across global market opportunities — global equity indices, fixed income, currencies, and commodities. The strategy is **broader than just trend or just carry** — it blends both with additional signals.

Per product page FAQ:

> "Portfolios are formed using proprietary quantitative innovations that emphasize characteristics such as, but not limited to: total return momentum, trends, seasonal patterns, carry measures, mean reversion and others, while simultaneously maximizing potential diversification based on changing estimates of volatility and correlations across global asset classes."

> "As portfolio weights and estimates of volatility and correlations change through time, the strategy will increase and decrease the gross exposure in an effort to maintain its target range of annualized portfolio volatility to control for tail risks."

Importantly, the macro stack will hold long or short positions in global equity index futures and Canadian Government Bond futures, dynamically increasing or decreasing the net exposure of the balanced sleeve.

### Operational details
- **Rebalances:** daily
- **No currency hedging** (the equity sleeve is global; FX exposure is part of the strategy)

## FAQs (from product page)

### How often does RGBM rebalance?
RGBM rebalances daily.

### How does RGBM get its exposure to a Balanced Allocation strategy?
The global equity portion can hold global equities, global equity ETFs, and global equity index futures. The Canadian bond portion can hold Canadian bonds, Canadian bond ETFs, and Canadian Government Bond futures. The systematic macro strategy will also hold long or short positions in these markets, increasing or decreasing net exposure.

### What is a systematic global macro strategy?
Invests in futures contracts to gain dynamic exposure across global equity indices, fixed income, currencies, and commodities. Uses proprietary quantitative innovations including momentum, trends, seasonality, carry, mean reversion, and others. Targets a range of annualized portfolio volatility to control tail risks.

### Is RGBM tax efficient?
Expected to be tax-efficient as part of a corporate-class structure. Consult a tax advisor.

### How often does RGBM make distributions?
**RGBM is not expected to make any distributions.**
