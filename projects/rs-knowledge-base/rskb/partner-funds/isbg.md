## Branding context

ISBG uses the **"IncomeSTKd"** brand — the income-focused variant of the "STKd" mark licensed from Newfound and ReSolve. Same licensing arrangement as BTGD.

## Investment case

For every $1 invested, ISBG provides **$1 of gold exposure** and **$1 of bitcoin exposure** — with **options-based premium income** layered on top.

Same hard-asset thesis as BTGD, but designed to **monetize implied volatility** through systematic options selling for income generation. The strategy targets investors who want both:
- The diversification and store-of-value characteristics of gold + bitcoin
- A regular income stream from the high implied volatility of the underlying assets

The base/stack labeling on the Return Stacked® ETFs site (which references ISBG as a partner fund) shows ISBG with **"100% Gold" as the base** and **"100% Bitcoin" as the stack** — in the order Quantify presents them.

## Mechanics

- Holds gold and bitcoin exposure (via futures, ETFs, ETPs, similar to BTGD)
- Layers an **options-selling overlay** on top to generate premium income
- Designed to produce monthly or regular income distributions from option premiums
