## Branding context (compliance-relevant)

BTGD uses the **"STKd"** brand mark, a derivative of Return Stacked®, under license. Per the BTGD product page:

> "Quantify Chaos Advisors, LLC ('Quantify') has entered into a brand licensing agreement with Newfound Research LLC ('Newfound') and ReSolve Asset Management SEZC (Cayman) ('ReSolve'), granting Quantify the right to use the 'STKd' brand, a derivative of Return Stacked®. Neither the Trust nor the Adviser is a party to this agreement. In exchange for the branding rights, Quantify will pay Newfound and ReSolve a fee based on a percentage of the Fund's unitary management fee."

This means:
- BTGD is a **Quantify product**, not a Return Stacked® product
- It carries the "STKd" brand to indicate its lineage from the return stacking concept
- ReSolve and Newfound earn licensing fees but do not advise the fund

## Investment case

For every $1 invested, BTGD provides **$1 of bitcoin exposure** and **$1 of gold exposure**.

Marketing positioning (verbatim from product page):
> "BTGD is the first-ever ETF to combine exposure to these two distinct asset classes while addressing traditional leveraged ETF flows through active management and innovative rebalancing. Leveraged for the long run."

Three pillars:
1. **Inflation Hedge** — could potentially help preserve purchasing power during inflationary periods.
2. **Currency Defense** — exposure to assets historically used as potential hedges against currency devaluation.
3. **Market Volatility Buffer** — gold's stability has the potential to offset bitcoin's volatility for a more balanced approach.

## Mechanics

- Actively managed exchange-traded fund
- Achieves exposure via:
  - **U.S.-listed futures contracts** (gold and bitcoin)
  - **Pooled investment vehicles (ETPs)** — referred to as "Underlying Funds"
- **Does NOT invest directly in spot bitcoin, digital assets, or gold bullion.**
- Cayman Subsidiary used (futures-based exposure)
- "Innovative rebalancing" addresses the typical decay issues of daily-leveraged ETFs by managing exposure differently (active management approach rather than daily reset)

## Compliance / risk notes

**BTGD-specific:**
- **BTGD does not invest directly in bitcoin or any other digital assets.** BTGD does not invest in or seek direct exposure to the current "spot" or cash price of bitcoin.
- **BTGD does not invest directly in gold or gold bullion.** Investors seeking direct exposure should consider an investment other than BTGD.
- **Bitcoin Investment Risk** — extreme volatility, regulatory risk, market sentiment driven
- **Gold Investment Risk** — futures + Underlying Funds, not physical
- **Digital Assets Risk, Blockchain Technology Risk**

**Risks applicable to all Quantify Funds ETFs:**
- Derivatives Risks, Leverage Risk, Futures Contracts Risk, Underlying Fund Risk, Cayman Subsidiary Risk, Potentially No 1940 Act Protections (some Underlying Funds), General Market Risk, New Fund Risk
