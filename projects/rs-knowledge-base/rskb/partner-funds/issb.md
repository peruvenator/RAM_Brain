## Branding context

## Investment case

For every $1 invested, ISSB provides **$1 of U.S. equity exposure** and **$1 of bitcoin exposure** — with **options-based premium income** layered on top.

Substitutes equity for gold (vs. ISBG) as the second exposure. Targets investors who want:
- Bitcoin exposure on top of (rather than in place of) U.S. equity exposure
- A regular income stream from options selling on the underlying assets

The base/stack labeling on the Return Stacked® ETFs site shows ISSB with **"100% U.S. Equity" as the base** and **"100% Bitcoin" as the stack**.

## Mechanics

- Holds U.S. equity and bitcoin exposure
- Layers an options-selling overlay for premium income generation
- Designed to produce regular income distributions from option premiums

(Detailed strategy mechanics — including which options structures are used and whether the overlay is on the equity side, the bitcoin side, or both — should be confirmed against the prospectus.)
